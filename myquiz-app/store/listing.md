**Full description** (max 4,000)
```text
MY QUIZ is a general-knowledge app for students preparing for competitive exams in India: learn, practise, understand, revise and compete, in Hindi or English.

Prepare every day
• Today's daily challenge: 10 questions, about 5 minutes
• Day streaks, a daily goal and reward points
• The most useful next step, based on your weak topics

Practise by subject
• Indian History, Geography, Indian Constitution, General Science, Sports and other subjects
• Questions by chapter and by level (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
• Filter by exam: UPSC, SSC CGL, Banking, Railways, State PSC and NDA/CDS

A full explanation for every answer
• Why the right answer is right, and why each other option is wrong
• Background, related facts and the source (such as NCERT)

Current affairs and Aaj ka Din
• Daily news with exam facts, and a quiz on them
• This day in history and important days, with their own questions

Mock tests and competition
• Timed mock tests with negative marking
• Build your own test, take the weekly challenge or challenge a friend
• Weekly and monthly leaderboards, winners and prizes

Make it stick
• Spaced repetition brings questions back at the right time
• Flashcards, bookmarks and a retry of your mistakes
• Progress by subject, accuracy and weak topics

Made for you
• Hindi or English, switched with one tap
• Light and dark mode, larger text
• Keep practising without internet
• Sign in with Google: your progress is safe on every phone

Privacy: the leaderboard shows only your display name and points. You can delete your account and all its data at any time from Profile.

Questions or ideas: [CONTACT EMAIL]
```
