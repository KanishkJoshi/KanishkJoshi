Privacy policy (English)

MY QUIZ is a general-knowledge learning app run by [YOUR COMPANY NAME] ("we"). This page explains what the app collects, why, where it is kept and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account details when you sign in: name, email address, profile photo and a user ID.
